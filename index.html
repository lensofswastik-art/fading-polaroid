A concept for getting friends to meet IRL

the fading
polaroid

Friend groups don't really break up. They just stop meeting. Nobody fights, nobody leaves the group chat. One day it's been four months. Drag time forward and watch the wall go grey. Then tap “we met up” and see the colour come back.

How I'd test it

Before building anything, I'd check whether people actually behave this way.

The human bitNobody notices a friendship fading. There's no moment, no fight. Just a slowly growing gap.
The ideaEvery hangout leaves one polaroid. Stop meeting and it goes grey. Meet again and the colour rushes back, with a little buzz in your hand.
Smallest testNo app. A shared album for 20 friend groups. I desaturate the photos by hand every week.
What I'd watchDoes anyone text the group “our photo is going grey, let's meet”? If yes, build it.
